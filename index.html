Welcome to XTRADE

XTRADE is a Bitcoin trading simulator with real-time price data. Make predictions based on actual BTC price movements and test your trading skills.

How It Works

  • Real Bitcoin Price: Uses live BTC/USD price from multiple APIs
  • 10-Second Trades: Choose LONG (price up) or SHORT (price down) and wait 10 seconds
  • Real Price Comparison: Win if your prediction matches actual price movement
  • No Probability: Wins/losses determined solely by actual BTC price direction
  • Cooldown System: 10 seconds wait between trades

Trading Rules

  • LONG: You win if Bitcoin price increases after 10 seconds
  • SHORT: You win if Bitcoin price decreases after 10 seconds
  • Draw: If price doesn't change, trade is canceled (no win/loss)
  • Payout: Bet Amount × Leverage (if win) / -Bet Amount × Leverage (if lose)
  • Cooldown: 10 seconds between trades to prevent spam

Note About Real Trading

This is a simulation game. Real Bitcoin trading involves much higher risk and market volatility. Never invest money you cannot afford to lose.
