Game Info

🚀 WELCOME TO XTRADE GAME

Experience the thrill of Bitcoin trading in this realistic simulation game! Test your skills, manage risk, and climb the leaderboards.

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

  • Start with $10,000 - Build your trading empire from scratch
  • Choose LONG or SHORT - Predict Bitcoin's price movement
  • Use Leverage - Multiply your profits (and losses!) from 1x to 100x
  • Set Stop Loss & Take Profit - Protect your capital and lock in profits
  • Track Real P&L - Watch your profit/loss update in real-time
  • Climb the Ranks - Compete with other traders on the leaderboard

🏆 GAME FEATURES

  • Real-time Bitcoin Price - Live BTC/USD feed with realistic fluctuations
  • Advanced Charting - Professional candlestick charts with indicators
  • Unlock System - Higher leverages unlock as your balance grows
  • Statistics Tracking - Monitor your performance with detailed stats
  • Risk Management Tools - Stop Loss, Take Profit, and position sizing
  • No Real Money Risk - Trade safely with virtual currency

💡 PRO TIPS

  • Start with low leverage (1x-3x) until you learn the game mechanics
  • Always use Stop Loss to protect your account from big losses
  • Risk only 1-5% of your balance per trade
  • Aim for a 2:1 or better Risk/Reward ratio
  • Higher leverage = higher risk! Use with caution
  • Watch the chart patterns before entering trades

⚠️ GAME OVER CONDITIONS

  • Your account reaches $0 - Game Over!
  • You can restart from $1,000 and try again
  • Your best balance is saved in the leaderboard
  • Risk management is key to survival

📊 LEVERAGE UNLOCKS

  • 1x: Novice (Starting level)
  • 2x: Beginner ($1,500 balance)
  • 3x: Intermediate ($2,500 balance)
  • 5x: Expert ($5,000 balance)
  • 10x: Risky ($10,000 balance)
  • 20x: High Risk ($25,000 balance)
  • 50x: Extreme ($50,000 balance)
  • 100x: Insane ($100,000 balance)

🏅 LEADERBOARD RANKS

Compete against other players and reach the top of the global rankings. Your best balance determines your position!

🎯 TRADING STRATEGIES

  • Trend Following: Trade in the direction of the current trend
  • Scalping: Take small profits quickly with high frequency
  • Swing Trading: Hold trades for longer periods for bigger moves
  • Risk Management: Focus on preserving capital above all else

📱 CONTROLS

  • Swipe Left/Right: Switch between control pages
  • Tap SL/TP Labels: Toggle Stop Loss/Take Profit on/off
  • Adjust Sliders: Set your risk parameters
  • Chart Controls: Zoom, change timeframe, add indicators

🎲 REMEMBER

This is a trading simulation game for entertainment and educational purposes. Real trading involves significant risk. Always educate yourself before trading with real money.

Good luck, trader! May the charts be with you! 📈
