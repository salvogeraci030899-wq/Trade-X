Welcome to XTRADE

Real-time Bitcoin trading simulator with ultra-fast price updates and 10-second trades based on ACTUAL Bitcoin price movements.

How It Works

  • Real Bitcoin Price: Live BTC/USD price with 250ms updates
  • 10-Second Trades: Choose LONG or SHORT and wait 10 seconds for result
  • Ultra-Fast Chart: Smooth 60FPS chart rendering
  • 100% Real Price: Win/Loss determined SOLELY by actual Bitcoin price movement
  • No Probability: Results are based on real market direction only

Trading Rules

  • LONG: Win if Bitcoin price INCREASES in 10 seconds
  • SHORT: Win if Bitcoin price DECREASES in 10 seconds
  • Trade Duration: 10 seconds - watch the chart in real-time!
  • Payout: Bet Amount × Leverage (win) / -Bet Amount × Leverage (lose)
  • Result: Based on ACTUAL price comparison (start vs end)
