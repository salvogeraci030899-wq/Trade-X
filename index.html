Welcome to XTRADE

Real-time Bitcoin trading simulator with realistic P&L calculation based on actual price movements.

How It Works

  • Realistic P&L: Profit/loss proportional to actual price movement
  • Real Bitcoin Price: Live BTC/USD price with 250ms updates
  • Open Trades: Trades remain open until manually closed or hitting Stop Loss/Take Profit
  • Stop Loss & Take Profit: Set automatic exit levels or disable them
  • Proportional Gains: Profit = Bet × (Price Change %) × Leverage

Trading Rules

  • LONG: Profit if Bitcoin price increases, loss if it decreases
  • SHORT: Profit if Bitcoin price decreases, loss if it increases
  • Trade Duration: Open indefinitely - close manually or with SL/TP
  • P&L Formula: Bet Amount × (Price Change %) × Leverage
  • Stop Loss: Automatic exit at specified loss % (optional)
  • Take Profit: Automatic exit at specified profit % (optional)

Controls

  • Toggle SL/TP: Tap on Stop Loss or Take Profit labels to enable/disable them
  • Close Trade: When a trade is active, use the CLOSE TRADE button

Risk Management

  • Risk/Reward Ratio: Take Profit % ÷ Stop Loss %
  • Leverage Multiplies: Both profits AND losses
  • Max Position Size: 100% of balance (use carefully!)
  • Margin Required: Bet Amount ÷ Leverage

Leverage System

  • 1x: Novice (unlocked)
  • 2x: Beginner (unlocks at $1,500)
  • 3x: Intermediate (unlocks at $2,500)
  • 5x: Expert (unlocks at $5,000)
  • 10x: Risky (unlocks at $10,000)
  • 20x: High Risk (unlocks at $25,000)
  • 50x: Extreme (unlocks at $50,000)
  • 100x: Insane (unlocks at $100,000)
