Welcome to XTRADE

XTRADE is a trading simulator with a dynamic progressive leverage system. Manage your capital, choose your risk level, and try to beat your record.

How It Works

  • Chart Analysis: Observe price trends and predict if it will rise (LONG) or fall (SHORT)
  • Dynamic System: Win probability depends on LEVERAGE and BET
  • Risk Management: Higher leverage OR bet means lower win probability
  • Personal Record: Your maximum record is saved and not reset in case of bankruptcy

Dynamic Probability System

Win probability is calculated as follows:

  • Base Probability: Depends on chosen leverage (from 80% to 20%)
  • Bet Penalty: Every 1% of bet reduces probability by 0.5%
  • Final Probability: Base Probability × (1 - (Bet% / 200))
  • Minimum Guaranteed: Never goes below 5%

Practical Examples

  • Prudent: Leverage 1x (80%) + Bet 5% → 78% win chance
  • Risky: Leverage 10x (40%) + Bet 20% → 36% win chance
  • Extreme: Leverage 100x (20%) + Bet 50% → 15% win chance
  • Suicidal: Leverage 100x (20%) + Bet 100% → 10% win chance

Unlock Thresholds

  • Leverage 2x: Unlocked at $1.5k
  • Leverage 3x: Unlocked at $2.5k
  • Leverage 5x: Unlocked at $5k
  • Leverage 10x: Unlocked at $10k
  • Leverage 20x: Unlocked at $25k
  • Leverage 50x: Unlocked at $50k
  • Leverage 100x: Unlocked at $100k

Strategic Advice

  • Start with low leverages and small bets to get familiar
  • Manage risk: high leverage + high bet = very low probabilities
  • To maximize expected value, balance leverage and bet
  • Never bet more than 20% with leverages above 10x
  • Remember: in real trading, capital management is everything!
